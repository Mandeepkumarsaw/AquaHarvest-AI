# Water Quality Norms and Health Guidelines (BIS 10500:2012 & WHO Norms)

1. RAW RAINWATER CHARACTERISTICS:
- pH: Typically 6.0 to 7.5 (slightly acidic in urban environments due to dissolved atmospheric CO2/SOx).
- Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): Extremely low, typically 20 to 80 ppm (soft water).
- Turbidity: High during initial rainfall surges (10-50 NTU) due to suspended soot, dust, and pollen; drops to < 5 NTU after proper first-flush diversion.
- Pathogens: Coliform bacteria may be present from avian feces and rodent traffic on rooftop catchments.

2. NON-POTABLE REUSE STANDARDS (TOILET FLUSHING & IRRIGATION):
- Turbidity: < 5 NTU.
- Fecal Coliforms: Nil detected per 100 ml for indoor flushing.
- Odor & Appearance: Unobjectionable, colorless.
- Treatment Required: Leaf screening + Slow sand filtration + Light chlorination (0.5 to 1.0 mg/L residual chlorine).

3. DRINKING / POTABLE REUSE CRITERIA (BIS 10500:2012):
- Rainwater must NOT be consumed directly without secondary multi-barrier disinfection.
- Required Treatment Train for Potable Use:
  a) Downpipe first-flush diverter (1.5 mm diversion).
  b) Dual-media quartz sand & activated granular carbon bed.
  c) 5-micron spun polypropylene cartridge sediment filter.
  d) UV (Ultraviolet) disinfection or Ozonation to achieve 99.99% microbial inactivation.
  e) Optional remineralization cartridge (calcium/magnesium) if TDS < 50 ppm.
