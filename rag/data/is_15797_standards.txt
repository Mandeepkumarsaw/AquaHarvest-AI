# Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) IS 15797:2008
# Code of Practice for Design and Installation of Rainwater Harvesting Systems in Buildings

1. SCOPE AND JURISDICTION:
- IS 15797 governs the planning, design, execution, and maintenance of rooftop rainwater harvesting structures for residential, commercial, and educational institutional campuses.

2. CATCHMENT & CONVEYANCE SYSTEM DESIGN:
- Gutters & Downspouts: Minimum gradient of 1:100 (1%) for rectangular and semi-circular gutters to prevent stagnant pooling and mosquito breeding.
- Material Specifications: UV-stabilized unplasticized PVC (uPVC) or galvanized iron (GI) piping conforming to IS 4985 / IS 1239.
- Sizing Norm: 1 sq. cm of downpipe cross-section per 1 to 1.5 sq. meters of roof catchment area, accommodating peak rainfall intensities of 50-75 mm/hr.
- Gutter Screens: Stainless steel or high-density nylon mesh screens (aperture 2 mm to 5 mm) at downspout inlets to stop foliage and tree twigs.

3. SETTLING & RETENTION TANK DESIGN:
- Tank Placement: Underground modular ferro-cement, reinforced cement concrete (RCC), or rotational molded food-grade polyethylene (HDPE).
- Overflow Provision: Minimum 150 mm diameter overflow pipe positioned at top water level (TWL), graded towards municipal storm network or ground recharge shaft.
- Vent Pipes: Insect-proof brass wire mesh cap over all air relief vents to prevent insect ingress.
- Cleaning Sump: Incline floor at 1:50 towards a central sludge drainage pit with submersible pump connection.

4. MAINTENANCE PROTOCOL:
- Pre-Monsoon Maintenance: Mandatory pressure washing of rooftop surfaces, clearing of downspouts, and replacement of top 5 cm filter sand layer before the onset of monsoon rains.
- First Rain Washing: The first heavy storm of the monsoon season must be completely diverted to waste.
